Śrī Maharudra Japanushtanam

MAHA RUDRA JAPANUSHTANAM · Rudra Japa & Rudrabhisheka
Culminating in Maharudra Havana on the 11th Day
An appeal to all devotees — many districts of Karnataka have not received the usual seasonal rains this year, leaving farmers unable to sow; in some other districts, recent excess rainfall has damaged the sowing already done. In such times, turning to the Divine in prayer is the path open to us. Shree Medha Dakshinamurthy Devasthana, on Magadi Road, Channenahalli, Bengaluru, is the manifest form of Lord Shiva who removes the many afflictions of devotees. By His grace, Janaseva Trust has resolved to conduct this Maharudra Anushthana, praying for timely rains and the prosperity of society. The Japa will continue from 3/8/2026 to 13/8/2026, culminating in the Maharudra Homa on 13/8/2026. We appeal to you to join this yajna — undertaken for the welfare of all — with your support of body, mind and wealth (tanu–mana–dhana), and to be a co-participant in this service to the Divine.
इमा रुद्राय तवसे कपर्दिने क्षयद्वीराय प्रभरामहे मतिम् ।
यथा नः शमसद् द्विपदे चतुष्पदे विश्वं पुष्टं ग्रामे अस्मिन्नातुरम् ॥
“We offer this prayer to mighty, matted-haired Rudra, lord of heroes, that it may be well with our bipeds and quadrupeds, and all in this land flourish, free from affliction.”
— Rudradhyaya (Śrī Rudram, Namakam 1.1)
